From a Local Seed Nursery to a Global Living Coalition

We started with a simple conviction: ecological restoration only endures when local communities hold genuine stewardship and open science leads the way.

2020

The First Seedlings in Sumatra

Founded by 12 field ecologists and 50 indigenous youths in North Sumatra. Working from a hand-built bamboo shade house, the team propagated and planted 5,000 critically endangered ironwood and meranti saplings to reverse illegal logging.

5,000 Indigenous Trees · 1 Community Nursery
2022

Grassroots Stewardship & Tech Tracking

Expanded across 35 village collectives. We established living-wage indigenous ranger patrols and pioneered our proprietary mobile seedling health ledger, verifying over 100,000 thriving trees with an unprecedented 89% two-year survival rate.

100K+ Trees · 40 Communities Empowered
2024

Blue Carbon & Coastal Sanctuaries

Bridged ridge-to-reef ecology with the launch of our Blue Carbon Sanctuary initiative. Restored 3,200 km² of degraded coastline, replanting over 200,000 mangroves and anchoring community coral nurseries to safeguard marine biodiversity.

3,200 km² Protected · 18 Marine Sanctuaries
2026 · TODAY

A Worldwide Living Coalition

Today, Sylvane unites 15,000+ donors, researchers, and village champions across 120+ communities. With more than 520,000 trees standing tall and self-regenerating forest corridors, we are proving that collective human will can heal the planet.

520K+ Trees · 120+ Communities · 100% For the Planet

Radical Transparency

Every dollar, GPS tree coordinate, and satellite mortality audit is published live on our public data ledger.

Community Sovereignty

Restoration is led by local indigenous stewards who make decisions, retain land rights, and lead monitoring.

Science-Backed Ecology

No monocultures. We plant diverse, native multi-strata species matched to indigenous soil micro-biomes.

TRUSTED BY GLOBAL ALLIANCES & INSTITUTIONS
