Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking visits, telehealth coverage, laboratory kits, and clinic appointments.

When clicking Book a Schedule or Book Visit on any doctor's profile, you can toggle between In-Clinic Outpatient Visit and Telehealth Virtual Video directly in the booking modal. Telehealth is ideal for initial assessments, prescription renewals, and non-emergency follow-ups, while in-clinic visits are recommended for physical examinations, ECG monitoring, and diagnostic imaging.

Every diagnostic kit includes sterile, painless sample collection materials, pre-paid return express courier packaging, step-by-step instructions, and a digital barcode to link your specimen to your Careon portal. Once returned to our CLIA-certified laboratory, digital pathology reports are delivered to your portal within 24 to 48 hours with a complimentary doctor review.

Careon Medical accepts major private insurance carriers, HSA/FSA cards, and self-pay plans. Transparent out-of-pocket estimates are calculated before you confirm your appointment. For self-pay consultations and home test kits, we support all major credit cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay with zero hidden facility fees.

Our Clinical Intelligence platform utilizes advanced anatomical rendering and sensor telemetry to map cardiac rhythm waveforms, arterial flow velocity, and genomic markers. Patients can explore high-resolution 3D reconstructions of their diagnostic scans alongside plain-language explanations from their attending specialist.

We understand that schedules change. You can reschedule or cancel any appointment up to 2 hours prior to your scheduled time without penalty directly from your appointment confirmation link or by calling our 24/7 patient support desk at +1 (800) 450-CARE.
