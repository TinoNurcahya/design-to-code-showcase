# 🏛️ Domain Archetypes & Layout Pacing Reference

> **Core Philosophy**: A world-class portfolio NEVER repeats the same layout skeleton. When expanding a Hero Section into a full landing page, the sections below the hero MUST organically reflect the **industry, narrative tone, and specific user value** of the product — not a generic "Bento -> How it Works -> FAQ" template.

---

## 📐 Layout Pacing & Rhythm Rules (Anti-Cookie-Cutter Rules)

1. **The Consecutive Layout Ban**:
   - **NEVER** place two identical grid structures adjacent to each other.
   - ❌ *Bad*: 3-column feature cards followed by 3-column pricing cards followed by 3-column testimonials.
   - ✅ *Good*: Asymmetric 2-column feature breakdown $\rightarrow$ Full-width interactive calculator $\rightarrow$ Horizontal scrolling lookbook $\rightarrow$ Compact telemetry stats bar.

2. **Visual Density Pacing (The Accordion Rhythm)**:
   - Alternate between **High Density** (data tables, code snippets, metric bentos) and **Low Density / Breathing Room** (editorial large typography, full-bleed media breaks, minimalist quotes).

3. **Domain-Specific Interactive Anchors**:
   - Every showcase page must feature at least **one domain-authentic interactive widget** (e.g. Currency slider, Menu filter, Code tab switcher, Card visualizer, Floorplan toggle).

4. **Sub-Niche Specialization (Same Industry ≠ Same Layout)**:
   - **Never treat an industry as a monolith**. In HealthTech, Telemedicine is completely distinct from AI Radiology SaaS or Bio-Wearable Rings. Each sub-niche addresses entirely different users, trust triggers, and product flows.
   - Always analyze the hero's headline, imagery, and UI cards to pinpoint the exact sub-niche before proposing sections.

5. **Visual DNA Inheritance (Deriving Geometry from the Hero)**:
   The visual geometry of the sections below the hero must inherit the composition structure of the hero:
   - **Asymmetric Split Hero (Text Left + Media Right)**: Body sections inherit alternating zig-zag editorial grids, pinned media showcases, and side-by-side comparative cards.
   - **Centered Monolith Hero (Centered Headline + Giant UI Preview below)**: Body sections inherit full-width ribbon breaks, 4-quadrant bento blocks, and centered interactive stages.
   - **Dark HUD Telemetry Hero (Dark mode + glowing waves/dials)**: Body sections inherit glassmorphic dark containers, interactive Canvas telemetry charts, and radial metric dials.
   - **Warm Tactile Lifestyle Hero (Soft pastels + rounded corners + organic photos)**: Body sections inherit gentle masonry grids, ambient breathing breaks, and warm testimonial callouts.

## 🎨 9 Domain Archetype Blueprints

### 1. 💳 Fintech, Web3 & Trading
- **Vibe**: Ultra-sleek, precision engineering, data-rich, high trust, dark/glass or Swiss high-contrast.
- **Narrative Flow**:
  1. **Sticky Header**: Brand logo, live market ticker badge, "Launch App" button.
  2. **Exact Sliced Hero**: Headline, dual CTA, floating 3D credit card / asset glass card.
  3. **Live Asset / Yield Ticker**: Realtime animated financial ticker with 24h change indicators.
  4. **Interactive Return / Fee Calculator**: Dual-range slider (Deposit amount & time period) with dynamic simulated earnings readout.
  5. **Security & Cryptographic Compliance Matrix**: 2-column asymmetric layout showcasing SOC2, multi-sig vault specs, and real-time uptime telemetry.
  6. **Interactive Card / Wallet Showcase**: 3D card tilt effect (CSS perspective) with theme switcher (Obsidian, Rose Gold, Platinum).
  7. **High-Security Pre-Footer & Compliance Footer**: Bank-level encryption disclaimers, license numbers, status indicator.

---

### 2. 👗 Luxury Fashion, Editorial & High Jewelry
- **Vibe**: Haute couture, expansive whitespace, refined serif/sans pairings, editorial asymmetry, silent luxury.
- **Narrative Flow**:
  1. **Minimalist Header**: Centered delicate wordmark, discreet seasonal menu, wishlist counter.
  2. **Exact Sliced Hero**: High-impact editorial model portrait or cinematic product still.
  3. **Curated Seasonal Lookbook**: Pinned horizontal scrub gallery or asymmetric staggered masonry layout.
  4. **Atelier Craftsmanship & Material Provenance**: 50/50 split screen — zoomed-in macro texture photography on left, evocative poetic typography & origin story on right.
  5. **Interactive Collection Showcase**: Filterable product shelf with instant Quick-View sliding drawer preview.
  6. **Press Quotes & Runway Critique**: Elegant large-type quotes with subtle horizontal line dividers (no avatar cards).
  7. **Refined Monospace Footer**: Bespoke concierge newsletter sign-up, boutique locations, copyright.

### 3. 🩺 HealthTech, Medical & Wellness (4 Sub-Niche Blueprints)

> **Important**: HealthTech is NOT a monolith. Two medical heroes must NEVER produce the same page layout. The hero's copy, imagery, and UI indicate which sub-niche it belongs to:

#### 3A. 📱 Telemedicine & On-Demand Virtual Care (Patient-to-Doctor App)
- **Vibe**: Accessible, empathetic, high-trust, friendly teal/sky tones, mobile-first hints.
- **Narrative Flow**:
  1. **Header**: Emergency hotline badge, language selector, patient portal button.
  2. **Exact Sliced Hero**: Telehealth promise, doctor portrait/preview, "Consult in 5 Min" CTA.
  3. **Instant Symptom & Specialty Filter Bar**: Interactive filter chips (General Physician, Pediatrician, Dermatologist, Mental Health) updating live doctor availability cards.
  4. **How a Virtual Visit Works**: 3-step interactive card tab (Pick Doctor $\rightarrow$ Video Consultation $\rightarrow$ Digital Prescription to Pharmacy).
  5. **Verified Clinician Carousel**: Doctor profile cards with education, ratings, languages spoken, and "Book Consultation" drawer trigger.
  6. **Insurance & Coverage Checker**: Interactive dropdown modal allowing patients to verify their health insurance provider coverage.
  7. **Emergency Hotline & HIPAA Compliance Footer**: Urgent care disclaimers, prescription delivery partners, pharmacy licenses.

#### 3B. 🔬 Clinical AI & Diagnostic MedTech SaaS (B2B Hospital / Radiologist Software)
- **Vibe**: High-precision, dark or sterile high-contrast, data-dense, technical monospace accents.
- **Narrative Flow**:
  1. **Header**: FDA clearance badge, institutional login, request hospital trial CTA.
  2. **Exact Sliced Hero**: High-res MRI/CT scan slice with AI bounding-box detection overlay and confidence score readout.
  3. **Interactive Scan Comparison Slider**: Interactive before/after split-slider comparing raw radiology scans with AI segmented pathology heatmaps.
  4. **Diagnostic Accuracy & ROC Curve Metrics**: Interactive data widget showing sensitivity vs. specificity benchmark against traditional diagnostics.
  5. **PACS & Hospital EHR Integration Architecture**: Flowchart diagram illustrating seamless DICOM ingestion with Epic/Cerner systems.
  6. **Peer-Reviewed Clinical Trials & Publications**: Filterable tab list of journal publications (Lancet, Nature Medicine) with DOI links and trial cohort sizes.
  7. **Institutional Trial Request Banner & Enterprise Regulatory Footer**: FDA 510(k) clearance status, CE mark, HIPAA/SOC2 compliance.

#### 3C. ⌚ Bio-Wearables & IoT Health Tracker (Hardware + Sensor Telemetry)
- **Vibe**: Sleek, lifestyle-athletic, dark mode with neon bioluminescent accents, industrial engineering.
- **Narrative Flow**:
  1. **Header**: Ring/Band model switcher, preorder CTA, cart drawer trigger.
  2. **Exact Sliced Hero**: Floating 3D smart ring or sensor device with live simulated pulse/HRV wave.
  3. **Interactive Sensor Anatomy & Exploded View**: Clickable device hotspots highlighting optical PPG sensors, temperature sensors, and titanium casing specs.
  4. **Live 24-Hour Telemetry Dashboard**: Interactive tabbed graph (Sleep Stages, Heart Rate Variability, Blood Oxygen, Recovery Score) updating dynamically.
  5. **Athlete & Clinician Testimonials**: Full-width video quote or dynamic audio playback badge from Olympic athletes or cardiologists.
  6. **Battery & Durability Spec Matrix**: 2-column comparative table showcasing 7-day battery life, 50m water resistance, and wireless charging dock.
  7. **Pre-Order Configurator Drawer & Specs Footer**: Finish selector (Matte Black, Brushed Silver, Stealth Gold) with size guide calculator.

#### 3D. 🧘 Mental Wellness, Mindfulness & Therapy
- **Vibe**: Soothing, soft organic curves, pastel sage/lavender tones, warm serif typography, zero-stress layout.
- **Narrative Flow**:
  1. **Header**: Crisis line discreet banner, therapist login, start journey CTA.
  2. **Exact Sliced Hero**: Calming illustration or lifestyle portrait with gentle ambient gradient animation.
  3. **Interactive Therapist Matching Quiz**: 3-step mini-quiz widget (Goal $\rightarrow$ Communication Style $\rightarrow$ Preferred Schedule) displaying matched therapist profiles.
  4. **Mindfulness & Audio Session Player**: Interactive audio player snippet for guided meditation with animated soundwave visualizer.
  5. **Daily Mood & Progress Tracker**: Interactive mood wheel / emotional check-in interactive demo.
  6. **Confidentiality & Privacy Shield**: Security highlight emphasizing end-to-end encrypted sessions and zero data sharing with employers.
  7. **Gentle Calming Pre-Footer & Support Footer**: 24/7 crisis support hotline, licensed therapist credential directory, privacy guarantees.

## 🧭 How to Select in Step 0 Alignment

When receiving a Hero Section:
1. **Identify the Domain**: Does the hero represent Fintech, Luxury, Medical, DevTools, Food, Real Estate, Studio, EV, or EdTech?
2. **Propose the Archetype**: State:
   > *"Berdasarkan hero reference, landing page ini bergenre **[Domain]**. Saya akan mengekstrapolasi alur halaman menggunakan Domain Archetype **[Nama Archetype]** dengan signature widget berupa **[Widget Interaktif]** agar tidak menghasilkan layout generik/template."*
3. **Confirm with User**: Provide the user with the proposed section outline before coding.
